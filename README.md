# CAT + IREN Investor Terminal Pro

## Update your existing website
Replace `app.py` and `requirements.txt` in your existing GitHub repository, then click **Commit changes**. Streamlit should automatically redeploy your existing website URL.

Includes two TradingView charts, adjustable intervals and styles, published analyst target consensus and implied upside/downside, daily RSI and moving averages, recent headlines, company financial metrics, opportunity/risk checklists, and historical price CSV downloads.

**Important limitations:** Embedded TradingView charts are not a paid TradingView API. Data may be delayed. Yahoo Finance quote, news, and analyst target data may be stale or missing and is separate from TradingView. Refreshing cannot guarantee second-by-second updates. Analyst estimates are aggregated and not attributed to individual firms. No autonomous AI forecast or breaking-news causal interpretation is claimed.
